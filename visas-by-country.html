By country of origin

Moving to Italy from your country

The Italian rules are the same for every non-EU national. What differs is where you apply, how your documents are legalised, which quota group you fall in, and the profiles that usually make the move.

India

Software, data and engineering; Blue Card first.

Brazil

Work permit or Italian citizenship by descent.

Turkey

Mechanical, industrial and textile specialists.

China

Embassy checklist, Hukou, CIMEA statement.

United States

Americans staying with their US employer.

Philippines

Health care, technical and support roles.

Not on the list? The same steps apply: see visa routes, or ask us about your nationality.
