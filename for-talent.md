# For Professionals Moving to Italy

> For non-EU professionals employed in Italy through ItalyBlueCard: your permit, your Italian contract, payslip, INPS pension, TFR, 13th month, leave and family.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">For professionals</span> <h1 id="you-are-moving-to-italy-with-us-as-your-employer">You are moving to Italy, with us as your employer</h1> <p class="lede">A company abroad wants you on its team, in Italy. We are your legal employer here: we sponsor your permit, sign your Italian contract and pay you every month. Your work, your manager and your goals stay with the company that chose you.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="your-permit">Your permit</h3><p>We file the nulla osta, sign the stay contract with you and track the renewal of your residence permit, so it never expires unnoticed.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="your-contract">Your contract</h3><p>A written Italian contract under the CCNL for your sector, with level, salary, hours and probation, in Italian and English.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="your-payslip">Your payslip</h3><p>Monthly, with tax and contributions already withheld. It is also the income evidence you need for a lease and for bringing your family.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="pension-and-health">Pension and health</h3><p>Your contributions go to INPS and build your Italian pension. You enrol in the national health service like any resident.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="tfr-and-13th-month">TFR and 13th month</h3><p>About 6.91% of your yearly pay set aside as TFR severance, and an extra month's pay in December (a 14th in summer under some CCNLs).</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="leave">Leave</h3><p>At least 4 weeks of paid holiday, public holidays, paid sick leave, maternity and paternity leave.</p></div> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="questions-from-people-on-the-move">Questions from people on the move</h2> <h3 id="who-is-my-employer-on-paper">Who is my employer on paper?</h3><p>ItalyBlueCard is your legal employer in Italy and the sponsor of your permit. The company abroad directs your daily work.</p><h3 id="what-if-i-want-to-change-job">What if I want to change job?</h3><p>With a Blue Card, in the first 12 months a change of employer needs prior approval of the labour office, deemed granted after 15 days without reply.
