# Relocation to Italy

> Your first weeks in Italy after the visa: stay contract, residence permit, tax code, residence registration, health service, housing and bank account.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Relocation</span> <h1 id="from-landing-to-first-payslip">From landing to first payslip</h1> <p class="lede">The visa gets you through the border. A handful of appointments in the first weeks turn it into a life in Italy. We handle the employer side of each one and tell you exactly what to bring.</p> </section> <section> <ol class="steps"> <li><strong>Stay contract</strong>You and your employer (us) sign the contratto di soggiorno at the Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione.</li> <li><strong>Residence permit</strong>Application through the postal kit, then fingerprints at the Questura. Keep the post office receipt: it shows the application is under way.</li> <li><strong>Tax code</strong>The codice fiscale is needed for the contract, payroll, a lease and a bank account.</li> <li><strong>Residence registration</strong>Registration at the municipal registry (anagrafe) at your address.</li> <li><strong>Health service</strong>Enrolment with the national health service and choice of a family doctor.</li> <li><strong>Bank account</strong>An Italian or EU account for your salary.</li> </ol>  </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="housing">Housing</h3><p>Rental contracts are usually long term. A temporary furnished flat for the first weeks gives time to choose a neighbourhood, and the final lease supports any family reunification request.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="documents">Documents</h3><p>Degree, marriage and birth certificates issued outside the EU need legalisation or apostille and a certified translation. A CIMEA comparability statement can replace the declaration of value for degrees.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="language">Language</h3><p>Offices work in Italian. We speak Italian and English and can come with you to the employer appointments.</p></div> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="relocation-service">Relocation service</h2> <p>Beyond the permit, we can organise the tax code, the housing search, health service registration and the bank account.
