# Probation in Italy When You Move on a Work Visa

> How the Italian probation period works, what it means for a relocated employee and their sponsor, and how to use it well on both sides.

Italian employment contracts usually start with a probation period, the *periodo di prova*. During probation either side can end the contract without notice and without giving a reason. After it, the job becomes much more stable. For someone who has crossed continents for the job, probation deserves some thought.

## The basics

- Probation must be **written in the contract** before work starts; otherwise it does not apply.
- Its **length is set by the CCNL**, by job level, up to **six months** for most roles.
- During probation, **either side can end** the relationship freely.
- After probation, the employee is confirmed automatically. Ending the job then requires a valid reason and the CCNL notice, or the employee's resignation.

## Why it feels different when you relocated

For a local hire, a failed probation is a setback. For a relocated employee, it can be much more: a lease signed, children enrolled in school, a permit tied to the job. Two things soften that risk.

First, the permit does not disappear overnight. As a Blue Card holder you can look for another job while unemployed, and the permit's validity is linked to the contract terms. We tell you exactly what applies in your case.

Second, probation is mostly a period to check fit, not a trap. Most professionals who relocate already know the company, often they already work for it. The probation then is a formality that the Italian contract needs, not a real test.

## Advice for the company employing you

- **Set clear goals for the probation.** Write them down and share them in the first week.
- **Do not leave the review to the last day.** Hold a mid-probation conversation. If something is wrong, say so early.
- **Remember the family.** If the employee's family is joining, a decision late in probation affects several people. Be honest early.
- **Do not use probation as a substitute for planning.** If the role is uncertain, consider a fixed-term contract instead, so expectations are clear.

## Advice for you

- Ask in advance what the probation goals are and who decides.
- Keep notes of the work you deliver in the first months.
- Do not over-commit financially in the first weeks: a temporary flat and a modest budget until probation ends is sensible.
- Plan the family move with the probation date in mind, unless you already know the company well.

## Probation and the permit timeline

Probation starts on your first working day in Italy, not on the date of the offer. If the consulate step takes longer than expected, probation simply starts later. Work done for the company from your home country before the move, under a different contract, does not count towards Italian probation.

## When probation ends without confirmation

The contract ends. You receive pay for the days worked, accrued 13th month, unused leave and the TFR accrued so far. As your employer we file the termination and tell you what it means for your permit and your options.

## When probation is passed

Nothing needs to happen: you are confirmed automatically. From then on, the Italian rules on dismissal apply. If the company later decides to end the arrangement, it needs a valid reason, and the CCNL notice applies.

## A note on prior service

If you have already worked for the company abroad for years, ask whether the probation can be shortened or waived. It is a reasonable request, and it signals the company's commitment.

## Last questions

**Can probation be extended?** Only within the CCNL limits and in specific cases such as long absences. It cannot be extended at will.

**Do I accrue leave during probation?** Yes, leave, 13th month and TFR all accrue from day one.

**Is probation shorter for junior roles?** Usually, yes. The CCNL sets lengths by level, with shorter periods for lower levels.

**Can I resign during probation?** Yes, without notice, like the employer. If you do, think about the permit first and talk to us.

**Does probation affect the permit application?** No. The permit is based on the contract, which includes probation as a normal clause. Probation starts only when you start work in Italy.

**Is probation the same for EU colleagues?** Yes. The CCNL applies to everyone at your level, whatever their nationality.
