# EU Blue Card for Italy

> EU Blue Card in Italy: who qualifies, the salary rule, the 30-day nulla osta deadline, permit length, changing jobs, family and EU mobility. With an Employer of Record (EOR) as sponsor.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Visa routes</span> <h1 id="the-eu-blue-card-in-italy">The EU Blue Card in Italy</h1> <p class="lede">The Blue Card (Carta blu UE) is Italy's permit for highly qualified employees. It is not subject to the annual quotas and has a legal deadline for the work authorisation. It still needs an Italian employer: if your company has none, we are that employer.</p> </section> <section> <h2 id="who-qualifies">Who qualifies</h2> <p class="prose">Under art. 27-quater, par. 1 of the Immigration Act (D.Lgs. 286/1998, as amended by D.Lgs. 152/2023), one of these is enough:</p> <div class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="a-degree">A degree</h3><p>A higher education qualification of at least three years, or a post-secondary qualification of at least three years (level 6 of the national framework).</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="a-regulated-profession">A regulated profession</h3><p>The recognition required for regulated professions (D.Lgs. 206/2007), for example in health care.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="five-years-of-experience">Five years of experience</h3><p>A higher professional qualification backed by at least five years of relevant experience at a level comparable to a degree.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="ict-three-years">ICT: three years</h3><p>ICT managers and specialists (ISCO-08 groups 133 and 25) with at least three years of relevant experience in the last seven years.</p></div> </div> </section> <section> <h2 id="contract-and-salary">Contract and salary</h2> <div class="prose"> <div class="fact"><b>6 months</b><span>The employment contract or binding job offer must last at least six months (par. 5, a).</span></div>  <div class="fact"><b>30 days</b><span>The immigration desk must issue the nulla osta, or notify a refusal, within 30 days of the application (par. 6).</span></div> <div class="fact"><b>2 years</b><span>For a permanent contract the permit lasts two years; otherwise the length of the contract plus three months (par. 11).</span></div> </div> </section> <section> <h2 id="after-you-arrive">After you arrive</h2> <div class="prose"> <ul> <li><b>First 12 months.</b> You do the job the card was issued for. Changing employer needs prior approval of the local labour office, considered granted if it does not answer within 15 days (par. 13).</li> <li><b>Losing your job.</b> You can look for and take up another job while unemployed (par. 13-bis).</li> <li><b>Side activity.</b> Self-employment is allowed alongside the qualified job (par. 13-ter).</li> <li><b>Equal treatment</b> with Italian nationals, apart from labour market access in the first 12 months (par. 15).</li> <li><b>Family.</b> Reunification is available whatever the length of your permit; family members get a permit of the same length (par. 16). See <a href="family-reunification-italy.html">family reunification</a>.</li> <li><b>Coming from another EU country.</b> A Blue Card from another Member State lets you work in Italy for up to 90 days in any 180; after 12 months there (6 if moving from a second Member State) you can move to Italy without a visa, with a work authorisation filed within one month of entry (par. 17).</li> </ul> <p><small>Source: art. 27-quater D.Lgs. 286/1998, <a href="https://brocardi.it/testo-unico-immigrazione/titolo-iii/art27quater.html">consolidated text</a>; joint circular Ministry of Labour - Ministry of the Interior no. 2829 of 28 March 2024 (<a href="https://lavoro.gov.it/priorita/pagine/carta-blu-ue-emanata-il-28032024-la-circolare-congiunta-del-MLPS-e-ministero-interno">lavoro.gov.it</a>). Read 4 Nov 2026.</small></p> </div> </section> <section> <h2 id="how-we-run-it">How we run it</h2> <ol class="steps"> <li><strong>Check</strong>Degree or experience, salary against the threshold and the CCNL, role code.</li> <li><strong>Contract</strong>We draft the Italian contract or binding offer, with your employer abroad setting the role and pay.</li> <li><strong>Nulla osta</strong>We file it as employer.
